# OPSORA — Feature List

OPSORA is a business management platform designed to centralize essential company operations, business information, finances, documents, and knowledge.

---

# 1. Dashboard

A central overview of the business.

## Features

- Monthly revenue overview
- Monthly expense overview
- Net cash flow
- Outstanding receivables
- Outstanding payables
- Active clients count
- Active deals count
- Recent financial activity
- Upcoming important dates
- Business alerts
- Quick access to key modules

---

# 2. Client Management

A centralized system for managing business clients and their information.

## Features

- Create and manage client profiles
- Company information
- Client contact details
- Multiple contacts per client
- Client status
- Business notes
- Client activity history
- Related documents
- Related deals
- Related invoices
- Payment history

## Client Overview

Each client should provide a complete business view containing:

- Client information
- Contacts
- Deals
- Documents
- Invoices
- Payments
- Notes
- Activity history

---

# 3. Sales Pipeline

Track potential business opportunities from initial contact to closure.

## Pipeline Stages

- Lead
- Discussion
- Proposal
- Negotiation
- Won
- Lost

## Features

- Create and manage deals
- Associate deals with clients
- Deal value
- Expected closing date
- Current pipeline stage
- Deal status
- Deal notes
- Related documents
- Proposal tracking
- Won and lost deal tracking
- Pipeline value overview
- Deal conversion metrics

---

# 4. Finance Ledger

A centralized financial tracking system for monitoring business income and expenses.

## Features

- Record income
- Record expenses
- Categorize transactions
- Track transaction dates
- Monthly finance overview
- Monthly income tally
- Monthly expense tally
- Net cash flow calculation
- Opening balance
- Closing balance
- Financial history
- Filter transactions by date and category

---

# 5. Invoice Management

Manage invoices generated by the company and invoices received from external parties.

## Features

- Create invoices
- Store generated invoices
- Record received invoices
- Link invoices to clients
- Track invoice amount
- Track payment status
- Due date tracking
- Mark invoices as paid
- Mark invoices as partially paid
- Mark invoices as overdue
- Outstanding invoice tracking
- Invoice history

## Invoice Status

- Draft
- Sent
- Partially Paid
- Paid
- Overdue
- Cancelled

---

# 6. Payments, Receivables and Payables

Track money coming into and leaving the organization.

## Receivables

- Track expected client payments
- Outstanding payment amount
- Payment due dates
- Payment status
- Client-wise receivables

## Payables

- Track money owed to vendors or external parties
- Payment due dates
- Payment status
- Expense-related payments

## Payments

- Record payments received
- Record outgoing payments
- Partial payment support
- Payment history
- Link payments to invoices

---

# 7. Document Management

A centralized document system for creating, generating, organizing, and storing company documents.

## Document Templates

- Create reusable document templates
- Markdown-based document editor
- Dynamic variables
- Template categories
- Edit templates
- Template version management

## Dynamic Variables

Example variables:

```text
{{client_name}}
{{company_name}}
{{date}}
{{amount}}
{{signatory_name}}
```

The system should replace variables with relevant data during document generation.

## Document Generation

- Generate documents from templates
- Fill dynamic variables
- Preview generated documents
- Save generated versions
- Download generated files
- Regenerate documents when required

## Document Storage

- Upload documents
- Store generated documents
- Organize documents by category
- Search documents
- Filter documents
- Version history
- Document metadata
- Related client or deal linking

## Document Categories

- Proposals
- Quotations
- Invoices
- MOUs
- Agreements
- Offer Letters
- Certificates
- Policies
- Internal Documents
- Other Business Documents

---

## Features

# MVP Feature Set

The initial version of OPSORA should focus on the following modules:

1. **Dashboard**
2. **Client Management**
3. **Sales Pipeline**
4. **Finance Ledger**
5. **Invoice Management**
6. **Payments, Receivables and Payables**
7. **Document Management**
8. **Document Vault**
9. **Company Knowledge Base**
10. **Business Alerts**

## Product Goal

> **OPSORA provides a centralized system for managing the company's clients, sales, finances, documents, and internal knowledge, with the flexibility to expand into a broader business operating platform over time.**
